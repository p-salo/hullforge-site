HullForge

Design a boat hull for its job, then print it watertight.

Give the payload, the speed and the size limits. HullForge generates the hull with the least power that meets them, checks that it floats with any one compartment flooded, and splits it into pieces for your printer with bolted, gasketed joints.

Hull generator

Monohull, catamaran, trimaran or pontoons. Hydrostatics, stability, wave and friction resistance, thrust and battery endurance — optimised, with every constraint shown met or missed.

Watertight print split

The fewest pieces that fit your bed, flanges with gasket grooves, heat-set insert bosses and bulkheads. Exports each piece as 3MF on the bed, with the screw and cord list.

Drones on water

Mounts for thrusters, dual-GNSS antennas, lidar and sonars; ArduPilot parameters and a ROS 2 URDF of the craft. And full parametric CAD for everything else you print.

Example: a 1.2 m catamaran for 15 kg of payload at 1.5 m/s splits into five pieces per hull on a 256 mm printer — 57 M3 screws, 2.5 m of gasket cord, about 2.8 kg of PETG a hull.
