Privacy: the early-access list

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who keeps the data

Panu Salo, Finland. Contact: hullforge@panu-automation.com.

What is kept, and why

  • Your e-mail address, to send you the HullForge trial when early access opens.
  • Which ad or link brought you to the page (for example "google" or "reddit"), to learn where interest comes from.

This page itself sets no cookies and has no analytics. The sign-up form inside it is Tally's (below).

The legal basis is your consent, given by submitting the form (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).

Who else sees it

The form service Tally (Tally BV, Belgium; stored in the EU) keeps the entries on our behalf. Nobody else gets them, and they are never sold or used for advertising.

How long

Until the trial invitation is sent, and at most 12 months; then the list is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask what is kept about you, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent at any time by writing to hullforge@panu-automation.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Finland the Data Protection Ombudsman, tietosuoja.fi).
